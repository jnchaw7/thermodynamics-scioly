DRAFT AREA IV

Heat Engines, Carnot Cycle & the Second Law

Heat-engine energy flows, efficiency, Carnot limit, refrigerators, and second-law statements.

Why it matters: The draft explicitly names cycles, engines, efficiency, and the first/second laws. Past tests often ask the Carnot sequence and engine efficiency.
Practice this rule areaJump to mini quiz

Heat engines

  • A heat engine absorbs Qh from a hot reservoir, produces net work W, and rejects Qc to a colder reservoir.
  • Over a complete cycle the working substance returns to its initial state, so ΔUcycle=0 and Wnet=Qh−Qc.

Efficiency and Carnot limit

  • Efficiency is the fraction of heat input converted to work: e=W/Qh=1−Qc/Qh.
  • No engine between fixed hot and cold reservoirs can exceed the reversible Carnot efficiency eC=1−Tc/Th.
  • Use kelvins in the Carnot formula.

Carnot cycle and second law

  • Carnot engine sequence: isothermal expansion at Th → adiabatic expansion → isothermal compression at Tc → adiabatic compression.
  • The second law can be stated in several equivalent ways: heat does not spontaneously flow cold→hot; a cyclic engine cannot convert all heat from one reservoir to work; isolated-system entropy does not decrease.

Formula / memory box

W = Qh − Qce = W/Qh = 1 − Qc/QheCarnot = 1 − Tc/Th

Worked example

Prompt

Engine absorbs 1000 J and rejects 600 J.

Solution

W=400 J; e=0.400=40%.

MINI QUIZ

Practice before you leave

Try each one without opening the answer. Then reveal it to self-check.

List the four Carnot steps in engine order.
Answer: Isothermal expansion, adiabatic expansion, isothermal compression, adiabatic compression.
Qh=1200 J, Qc=800 J. Find W.
Answer: 400 J.
Using the same values, find efficiency.
Answer: 33.3%.
Carnot engine between 600 K and 300 K: efficiency?
Answer: 50%.
Can a cyclic heat engine convert all heat from one reservoir into work?
Answer: No; that violates the second law.
Why does an open refrigerator door warm a closed room overall?
Answer: It rejects the heat it removes plus the electrical work input back into the room.
